## Confirmed policies and current assumptions

The user confirmed that Paid leave earns salary, advance recovery need not be capped at salary, and negative payout must remain signed and tracked. Actual outstanding debt remains the recovery ceiling. Payroll shortfall and wallet overdraft tracking are both implemented under the current scope assumption; owner funding/withdrawal classification is still separate. Historical assertions based on other assumptions retain their original results and must be revised against the confirmed contract before verification claims.

| Policy | Current implementation | Confirmation and evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid and unpaid leave | [Shared drafts](../../server/src/modules/payroll/drafts.js) count employee-matched attendance linked to Approved Paid leave as one salary day, otherwise Present=1/Half-Day=0.5; Unpaid earns zero. [Leave approval](../../server/src/modules/leaves/controller.js) retains linked Absent attendance and protects overlap. | **Confirmed:** Paid leave earns salary. Entitlement/accrual rules remain separate. Historical LEAVE-006 failure is preserved in [hr-first](../audit/phase2/fixes/hr-first/results.json); the mixed attendance/paid/unpaid case is defined in [completion tests](../../tests/audit/completion.spec.cjs), executed successfully in the final full audit, with remaining scope in coverage.csv. |
| Recovery limit and negative salary | Both APIs use shared drafts: full outstanding debt by default, explicit recovery bounded by debt, signed net. [Lifecycle](../../server/src/modules/payroll/lifecycle.js) posts recovery and a shortfall in account 1110 for negative net; [settlements](../../server/src/modules/payroll/shortfalls.js) collect cash and require receipt reversal before payroll correction. | **Confirmed:** recovery may exceed salary and negative payout is tracked. Salary 30,000/debt 40,000 gives net −10,000, recovery 40,000 and shortfall 10,000. The historical PAY-004 salary-cap expectation is superseded as a requirement, while its result remains preserved. Completion PAY-004/010/011 defines settlement/reversal/repayout/deletion checks; these named subcases pass the final full audit; broader coverage remains partial. |
| Wallet overdrafts and owner transfers | [TransferService](../../server/src/modules/finance/TransferService.js) retains signed balances and records transfer direction, before/after balance and actor; GET history returns overdraft magnitude. User transfers do not reject insufficient wallet funds. Owners may have Equity accounts. | **Current assumption:** track payroll and wallet negative balances. Historical TRANSFER-003 no-overdraft failure in [finance-first](../audit/phase2/fixes/finance-first/results.json) is not the current tracking requirement. Owner funding/withdrawal classification remains unsettled. Employee-overdraft history and summaries filter to Asset accounts, so Equity accounts receive no employee-overdraft label. |

The missing-attendance full-month fallback remains current source behavior, now stored/shown explicitly. The routed Payroll form uses nullish days fallback and parseFloat; the explicit zero/fractional-day browser subcase passes the final full audit. Import status synchronization refuses missing/failed/no-matched attendance and logs actual changes only. These facts do not approve a broader attendance or import policy.
